I strongly support the motion that there should be strict laws to regulate LLMs.

LLMs are not just harmless tools; they are powerful systems that can influence information, decisions, and behavior at massive scale. When a technology can generate convincing falsehoods, enable fraud, spread bias, and manipulate public opinion, leaving it largely unregulated is irresponsible. The risks are not theoretical—they are already happening.

First, strict regulation is necessary to protect the public from harm. LLMs can be used to produce scams, impersonation, deepfake-style text, and large-scale misinformation with alarming ease. A single bad actor can now automate deception at a scale that would have been impossible before. Without strong legal guardrails, ordinary people become the victims.

Second, LLMs can amplify discrimination and unfairness. These models are trained on human data, which means they can reproduce bias in hiring, lending, healthcare, education, and policing contexts. If companies are allowed to deploy them freely without legal accountability, then biased outputs can quietly become institutionalized harm.

Third, strict laws are essential for accountability. Right now, when an LLM causes damage, responsibility is often blurred between developers, deployers, and users. That is unacceptable. Laws should clearly require transparency, independent testing, auditability, and liability when negligence causes harm. Powerful systems without clear responsibility invite abuse.

Finally, regulation does not mean stopping innovation. It means ensuring innovation is safe, trustworthy, and in the public interest. We regulate medicines, airplanes, and food because their benefits are too important to leave ungoverned. LLMs now belong in that category of high-impact technology. If they are powerful enough to shape society, they are powerful enough to require strict legal oversight.

In short, strict laws are not an obstacle to progress—they are the foundation of responsible progress. Without them, we risk turning a transformative technology into a tool of manipulation, harm, and unchecked power.
